# RIVQ — Launch Announcements & FAQ

Honest, clear, student-focused launch copy. No emojis, no unsubstantiated hype ("open, decentralized, zero-noise, permanent"), no unproven claims, and no mention of Google Play until the app is approved and published.

---

## 1. LinkedIn Post (Founder Announcement)

```text
Finding teammates for hackathons and research projects has always been broken. Most groups end up on random WhatsApp chats, and half the team goes unresponsive before submission day.

I built RIVQ (https://synqsquad.vercel.app) to make teaming up straightforward for college students and independent developers.

You can post what you are building—like a Smart India Hackathon project or a conference paper—and list the specific roles you need. Anyone interested connects with you directly over Gmail using their verified Google account. There are no built-in chat silos or spam feeds; conversations move straight to email.

If you are currently preparing for hackathons or looking for a co-author, try posting your requisition or adding your talent card to the directory: https://synqsquad.vercel.app

Feedback and critiques are welcome.
```

## 4. Five-Line FAQ

1. **What is RIVQ?**
   RIVQ is a lightweight directory where student developers and researchers can find teammates for hackathons, research papers, and software projects.

2. **How do I connect with someone?**
   Sign in with your Google account, click "Connect" on any open project or collaborator profile, and RIVQ will open a prefilled draft in Gmail so you can talk directly.

3. **Does RIVQ charge any fees?**
   No. RIVQ is completely free to use for students, researchers, and builders.

4. **How is my email and data handled?**
   Your email is kept private from unauthenticated viewers and is only revealed when you initiate or receive a direct collaboration request. You can export or delete your profile and all associated data at any time under Settings.

5. **Is there an Android app?**
   RIVQ currently runs in your mobile browser as a fast Web app. An Android app is currently in closed testing and will be available once Play Store verification is complete.
