[FULL DESCRIPTION] (Maximum 4000 characters)
SynqSquad is a focused teammate discovery platform built for university students, independent developers, and academic researchers.

When tackling a major hackathon (such as Smart India Hackathon or ETHGlobal) or drafting an academic paper (for conferences like NeurIPS, ICRA, or ACL), the hardest challenge is rarely the idea—it is finding one reliable person with the exact complementary technical skill you need.

SynqSquad solves this with two-way discovery:
1. Person → Team: Browse open requisitions for hackathons and research projects and connect directly.
2. Team → Person: Explore the Talent Directory to discover skilled builders and researchers open to collaborate, and invite them to your squad.

One clear principle: one person finds one collaborator, and you communicate directly on verified Gmail. There are no closed proprietary inboxes, no chat channels to manage, no bot spam, and no algorithmic hype.

Key Features:

1. Two-Way Discovery (Requisitions & Talent Profiles)
Explore active hackathon and research paper requirements or browse availability profiles in the Talent Directory. Filter by event, domain (Machine Learning, Systems, Web3, Full-Stack, UI/UX), or specific technology stack.

2. Verified Google Authentication & Privacy by Default
Every user signs in with a verified Google account. To prevent public scrapers and data harvesters from abusing creator contacts, raw email addresses are masked across all public feeds. A collaborator's unmasked email is revealed only when an authenticated peer connects.

3. Direct Gmail Outreach
Connecting opens a pre-filled, editable draft directly in your Gmail compose window with relevant context from the posting. We also provide direct clipboard copy and standard local mail client links.

4. Fast Requirement & Availability Publishing
Publish a team requisition or a personal talent availability card in under 30 seconds. Postings automatically expire after 30 days to keep the directory fresh, with simple one-click renewal.

5. Spam Protection and Rate Limiting
To ensure collaborators receive genuine inquiries rather than mass solicitations, accounts are limited to 10 Gmail outreach connections per 24-hour rolling window across all interactions.

6. Built-in Safety & Content Moderation
Users can report requisitions or talent profiles for spam or inappropriate content and instantly block members. Blocked users' content is immediately hidden in both directions across search and feeds.

7. Full User Control & Data Rights
Under India's DPDP Act 2023 and GDPR, you have complete sovereignty over your data. Download a machine-readable JSON export of your profile, listings, and outreach history anytime, or erase your account and all associated records permanently in one click.

Whether you need a systems programmer for an embedded robotics hackathon or a co-author experienced in PyTorch for an ML manuscript, SynqSquad helps you find your teammate with zero friction.
